### **Step 1: Market Research**
1. Go to Admin Dashboard → "Market Intel" tab
2. Search for activity: "desert tour", "hot air balloon", etc.
3. System fetches competitor prices from multiple sources
4. Shows market analysis with average prices and opportunities

### **Step 2: Competitive Analysis**
- **Average market price**: Shows what competitors charge
- **Price range**: Min and max competitor prices

- **Top competitors**: Shows which platforms dominate

### **Step 3: Smart Pricing**
- **Our competitive price**: Automatically calculated 20% below average
- **Customer savings**: Shows how much customers save vs competitors

- **Profit margin**: Maintains 30% profit margin

### **Step 4: Add Activity**
- **One-click addition**: Add activity with competitive pricing
- **Automatic pricing**: System suggests optimal price
- **Competitor tracking**: Keeps track of competitor prices
- **Market intelligence**: Stores market data for future reference
